ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Dibya Jyoti Secondary School

Dibya Jyoti Secondary School is a respected community-based educational institution located in Chisapani, Bardaghat-02, Nawalparasi West, Lumbini Province, Nepal. Established in 1977 AD (2034 BS), the school has been contributing to the educational and social development of the local community for many decades.

The school believes that education is not only about academic achievement but also about developing responsible, disciplined, creative, and capable individuals. With a focus on quality education and holistic development, Dibya Jyoti Secondary School provides learning opportunities from Early Childhood Development through Grade 12.

The school is affiliated with the National Examinations Board (NEB) for its secondary and higher-secondary programs.

Higher Secondary Programs

At the higher-secondary level, the school provides +2 programs in Science, Management, Humanities, and Education, giving students opportunities to select academic pathways according to their interests, abilities, and future goals.

Learning Environment

Dibya Jyoti Secondary School strives to create a supportive and engaging learning environment where students can develop their knowledge, skills, confidence, and character. The school emphasizes effective teaching and learning while encouraging students to participate actively in academic, co-curricular, and extracurricular activities.

Facilities & Activities

The school has a range of educational and extracurricular facilities, including a library, science laboratory, computer laboratory, multimedia resources, classrooms, sports activities, music and dance programs, educational tours, counseling, scholarships, and other extracurricular activities.

These facilities and activities help students gain practical knowledge and develop their talents beyond the classroom. Student participation is an important part of school life, with academic programs complemented by cultural performances, sports, creative activities, and other co-curricular opportunities.

Our Role in the Community

As a community school, Dibya Jyoti Secondary School plays an important role in making education accessible to students in and around Chisapani and Bardaghat. The school also provides scholarship opportunities for deserving students, helping encourage learners to continue their education and pursue their ambitions.

Our Journey

Over the years, Dibya Jyoti Secondary School has become an important educational institution in the area. Its long history, community-oriented approach, broad academic programs, and emphasis on students' overall development reflect its commitment to preparing young people for higher education, employment, and responsible citizenship.

The school looks toward the future with the goal of continuously improving the quality of education, strengthening its learning environment, supporting teachers and students, and responding to the changing needs of society.

Our Commitment

At Dibya Jyoti Secondary School, we are committed to providing an educational environment where every student is encouraged to learn, explore, create, participate, and grow.

We aim to nurture knowledgeable, skilled, confident, disciplined, and socially responsible citizens who can contribute positively to their families, communities, and nation.

“Empowering students through quality education, character, knowledge, and opportunity.”
