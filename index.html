ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Learning Today, Leading Tomorrow.

Dibya Jyoti Secondary School is a public/community educational institution located at Chisapani, Bardaghat-02, Nawalparasi West, Nepal.

The school provides education from early childhood through Grade 12 and also offers Plus Two programs affiliated with the National Examination Board.
